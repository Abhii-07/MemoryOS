## Decisions taken

- Eviction = physical purge (privacy invariant #2); `status='decayed'` is
  soft and untouched by lineage; `status='merged'` keeps raw records in place.
- Closure walk is BFS-over-GIN + depth resolution; rebuild order = ascending
  (lineage depth, → id) — deterministic on any machine (R8).
- Depth cap `d > 4` evicts instead of rebuilding (ADR-005 revisit condition
  made concrete); cascade > `max_sync_derived` (100) → 202 job.
