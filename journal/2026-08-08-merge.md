## 3. Falsification & baseline record

The first real baseline run (D3) falsified the naive design: recall@5 1.0
but sensitive-leak 1.0 on both retrieval and injection paths, contradiction
rate 0.33, cold-start false-positive 0.5, task pass 0.67. The productive
failure report (`experiments/naive_baseline/productive_failure_report.md`)
records that the baseline must not be accepted as a solution — it is the
measured gap the D4+ design must close. End-of-merge rerun (see §5) confirms
reproducibility.
